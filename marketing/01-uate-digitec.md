### English (for the program, if they ask)

**Title:** "From question to the letter of the law in 60 seconds: AI that
doesn't make things up"

**Format:** 15–20 min, live demo. Needs: screen + internet.

**Abstract (~120 words):**

LLMs hallucinate with confidence, and in law a hallucinated answer is the
worst possible failure. This talk shows how to build an AI system that
answers *only* from stored legal text: quotes are extracted
programmatically — verbatim, never regenerated by the model — and when the
law doesn't cover the question, the system says so outright.

We take a real tax question from the audience and show the full path on
screen: question → retrieval over Armenian legislation → answer with a
verbatim article quote and an ARLIS link, in under a minute.

The audience leaves with three things: why grounding is an architecture
problem, not a prompt; what building retrieval for a low-resource language
like Armenian teaches you; and a measurable evaluation method that applies
to any AI product.

**Speaker:** Rafayel Arakelyan, founder of MatyanAI. Used by accountants
with 20–25 years of practice; evaluated against a golden set of real
professional questions.
